# Praxedis Technologies - Website Design Document

## Vision
A professional, disruptive, and vibrant website for Praxedis Technologies (`www.praxedistechnologies.com`), run by Pedro M. Dominguez. The site will highlight the unique value of a single operator performing at "Mag 7" speed, inspired by the revolutionary spirit of Praxedis G. Guerrero.

## Aesthetics
- **Theme:** Vibrant Mexican Heritage meets High-Tech Professionalism.
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Mexican Green:** `#006847` (Primary Accents, Buttons, Headers)
  - **Mexican Red:** `#CE1126` (Action items, highlights, borders)
  - **White:** `#FFFFFF` (Clean backgrounds)
  - **Off-White/Light Gray:** `#F4F4F4` (Section backgrounds)
  - **Dark Gray/Black:** `#1A1A1A` (Text, Professional contrast)
- **Typography:**
  - **Headers:** Bold, sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat) to convey strength and authority.
  - **Body:** Clean, highly readable sans-serif (e.g., Inter).
- **Visual Elements:**
  - Subtle geometric patterns inspired by Mexican textiles or architecture.
  - High-contrast sections to emphasize speed and disruption.

## Content Structure
1.  **Hero Section:**
    - Headline: "One Person. Mag 7 Speed."
    - Sub-headline: "Praxedis Technologies: Disrupting the industry through the vision and execution of Pedro M. Dominguez."
    - Call to Action: "Experience the Disruption."
2.  **The Disruptor (About Pedro):**
    - Section explaining the "Full-Stack" nature of the company (Developer, Marketer, Analyst, Programmer).
    - The philosophy of lean, high-velocity operation.
3.  **The Heritage (Praxedis G. Guerrero):**
    - A tribute to Praxedis G. Guerrero (Chihuahua, Mexico).
    - Connecting the revolutionary spirit of the past to the technological disruption of the future.
4.  **Capabilities (The Stack):**
    - Highlighting full-cycle delivery: From data analysis to marketing and deployment.
5.  **Contact/Footer:**
    - Professional footer with the company name and links.

## Implementation Plan
1.  Create `index.html` with a modern, semantic structure.
2.  Create `main.css` with a responsive design and the Mexican heritage color palette.
3.  Include high-quality typography and interactive elements (hover states, smooth scrolling).
